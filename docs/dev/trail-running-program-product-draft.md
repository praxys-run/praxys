## Proposed promise and value

Scope covers adults with a running base in nonclinical settings, including daily
training without an event, first-finish and performance goals. Single-day events
include exactly **50,000 metres**. Eligibility still depends on experience,
expected duration, ascent, descent, technical terrain and environment. Recording
a 50 km goal does not establish complete race preparation support.

Resources are editable and a gym is not required. Resources, equipment,
familiarity, history and actual dates are separate. Same-day running and strength
have independent completion; each proposal covers 14 days with a day-7 review.
Science's reported-history path, home exercises, controlled uphill work,
familiar-terrain maintenance, dual sessions, progression / regression, run / walk,
fueling practice and taper remain candidates with applicability limits. The exact
SDR / contract governs their doses.

The minimum value path is: set conditions → understand support and gaps → review
executable sessions → explicitly adopt → complete and give feedback per session →
review. The interface distinguishes “Supported under these conditions”, “Included
in this proposal” and “Scheduled”. Local gaps may preserve independently qualified
modules; global eligibility or stop conditions still block globally.

Complete race preparation remains the product goal. Missing first-50-km admission,
long-run / back-to-back doses, vertical and technical progression, novel descent,
negative treadmill, performance / pace progression, complete race nutrition and
environment prescriptions cannot meet acceptance through partial module support.
The interface continues to show “Race preparation remains incomplete”, without
promising injury prevention, finishing or personal safety.

Product proposes accepting Architecture / Trust's tradeoffs for validity,
confirmation on each proposal, stable identities, transactions, client
compatibility and deletion of all derivative copies. Bounded retention gives up
indefinite replay; retention periods, export and deletion must be explained before
adoption. This support is a proposal opinion; specialist boundaries still need
independent acceptance.

## Acceptance dependencies and observation

P1 scientific claims and core candidates have undergone [independent Science
review](trail-running-program-independent-science-review.md), with no unresolved
review findings in the revised version. This does not fill the prescription gaps
above or replace human content acceptance, final role-version checks or independent
Quality. Historical accepted v2 and draft / inactive v3 remain unchanged.

After the relevant decisions are accepted, P2 / P3 can implement identity,
compatibility, resources, provenance and rights together. P4 / P5 await exact
prescriptions per module; P6 awaits transaction and authorization contracts;
P7 awaits Experience and rendered verification; P8 separately requires Operations
and release authority. See the [impact and blocker
matrix](trail-running-program-implementation-impact.md).

Success requires every promise to map to exact rules, exclusions and acceptance
scenarios. Guardrails prohibit invented history or dates, false preparation
completeness, completed-record loss, cross-owner reads, stale adoption and provider
calls. Future Product observation uses voluntary feedback on the 14-day journey
and day-7 review, without new training-value telemetry. Repeated unschedulable city
conditions or misunderstanding of partial coverage should revise the hypothesis;
a usable Ninghai path does not prove efficacy or safety.
